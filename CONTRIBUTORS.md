# Contributors

Pocket Relay credits people and organizations who make **app-owned**
contributions under the project copyright policy. Third-party packages and
their authors are credited separately in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)
and the generated SBOMs.

| Contributor | Role | Copyright status |
|---|---|---|
| Pocket Relay Maintainer | Initial project steward | Copyright holder for the initial app-owned source, as represented by the repository owner. |

## Adding contributors

Maintainers add a contributor here after merging a substantial app-owned code,
documentation, design, or testing contribution. Listing someone is an
attribution record; it neither transfers copyright nor replaces the
contributor certification in [COPYRIGHT_POLICY.md](COPYRIGHT_POLICY.md).

Contributors who prefer a different public attribution name should state that
in their merge request. Do not add private contact information without the
person's consent.
